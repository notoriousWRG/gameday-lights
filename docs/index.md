# Gameday Lights

When your team scores, a light in your room flashes their colors, at the moment you see the
score on your TV, not before it.

It runs quietly on a computer you already have at home. It checks ESPN for your team's games,
watches them while they're on, and flashes a smart bulb (WiZ or Govee) when your team scores.
It waits a few seconds first, so a streamed picture that runs behind live TV doesn't get
spoiled. Everything is set up from a web page on your own network. There are no accounts, and
nothing about you is sent anywhere.

## What you need

- A computer that can stay on during games: a Mac, a Windows PC or a Linux box
- A **WiZ** or **Govee** smart bulb or strip, already on your home Wi-Fi
- About 15 minutes

## Get started

1. **[Install it on your Mac](install-mac.md).** One line pasted into Terminal.
2. **[Set it up](first-setup.md):** find your bulb, pick your team, press Test.
3. **[Line it up with your TV](game-day.md):** a few minutes before a game, so the light doesn't spoil a play.

Using Windows or Linux? Those install pages are coming.

## If something goes wrong

Start with [When something is wrong](troubleshooting.md). Its last section shows how to copy a
short report to text to whoever set this up for you.
