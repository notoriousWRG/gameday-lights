      Calibrate the delay

      Streaming runs behind live TV, so the lights wait a few seconds after a score before they fire. To find the right wait, watch a game: when a score shows on your TV, press the button on that score. The gap between the app noticing it and you seeing it is your lag. Each reading includes the app's own polling time, exactly as a real flash does.
